# Contract: Collector certificates and client authentication

> Shared contract. Read only if your module's task table lists it. Do not change without the user's approval; see the contract index in the root `CLAUDE.md` for consumers.

- **PoC CA:** self-signed, EC P-256, CN `MOD PoC Collector CA`, stored as Key Vault certificate `collector-ca` (exportable). Created by infra. Management-api signs with it; ingest-api and management-api validate against its public certificate.
- **Collector key:** EC P-256, generated by the collector on first run, stored under `{DATA_DIR}/identity` (PoC: file, not TPM).
- **CSR:** PKCS#10 PEM. Subject is ignored; the server sets it.
- **Issued certificate:** subject `CN={collectorId}` (lowercase GUID); SAN URI `urn:mod:collector:{collectorId}`; EKU clientAuth; KeyUsage digitalSignature; valid from now − 5 min for `CERT_VALIDITY_DAYS` (default 7); random 16-byte serial.
- **Thumbprint** everywhere in the system: SHA-256 of the DER encoding, lowercase hex, 64 chars.
- **Presenting:** the collector presents its certificate as the TLS client certificate on every call to ingest and management endpoints except `POST /v1/enrol`.
- **Server-side extraction:** Azure Container Apps forwards the client certificate in the `X-Forwarded-Client-Cert` header as `;`-separated fields including `Hash`, `Cert` (URL-encoded PEM) and `Chain`. Parse the `Cert` field. Confirm the exact header format against current Azure Container Apps documentation during implementation.
- **Validation** (ingest-api and management-api): chains to the CA public certificate using custom trust (no system store); within validity; CN parses as GUID; SAN matches CN; a `registry.Certificate` row exists for the thumbprint with `RevokedAt` null; the collector's `Status` is `Enrolled`. Registry lookups may be cached for at most 30 s, so revocation takes effect within 30 s.
- **Renewal:** the collector renews when less than 48 h of validity remain.
